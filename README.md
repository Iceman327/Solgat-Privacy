# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 8, 2026**

Solgat Studio ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you play our VR titles.

## 1. Information We Collect
* **Telemetry & Performance Data:** We may automatically collect standard diagnostic information, crash reports, and performance metrics to ensure the game functions properly on your device. 
* **Multiplayer & Session Data:** If you play online multiplayer, our servers process basic network positioning (such as packed positional states necessary for gameplay) and connection identifiers.
* **Device Permissions:** The game accesses standard VR tracking data (headset and controller orientation/position) solely to render your virtual avatar and gameplay environment locally. We do not record or store raw biometric video feeds or environmental camera pass-through data on external servers.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected data strictly to:
* Provide, maintain, and improve our multiplayer and game systems.
* Diagnose crashes, optimize frame rates, and debug network performance.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal user data to outside commercial third parties. 

## 4. Data Deletion Requests
Because we do not store persistent personal user profiles or sensitive personal records, you can clear local application state at any time by uninstalling the game. For any specific inquiries or data deletion requests, you can reach out directly via our contact support channel.
